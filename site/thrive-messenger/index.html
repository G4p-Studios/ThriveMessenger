Thrive Messenger

Thrive Messenger is an accessible direct, group, file, and voice messaging client for hosted and self-hosted Thrive servers.

The TappedIn distribution connects to im.tappedin.fm by default and adds server administration, group rooms and roles, direct and group voice, secure modular features, updates, and Apple-platform support.

This distribution is based on the original open-source Thrive Messenger project by G4p Studios. G4p Studios does not operate or endorse the TappedIn service unless it says so independently.
